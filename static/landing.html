Built for documents that matter.

Contracts, policies, papers, manuals. The kind of reading where "probably" is not good enough.

01

Scanned pages, read.

Image-only PDFs are detected automatically and read with OCR, then searched like any other page. Highlights land on the scan too.

02

Ask in any language.

Ask in Turkish about an English policy. The answer comes back in the language you asked, citing the original wording.

03

Cited, or it says nothing.

Each claim carries a page reference. When the document does not contain the answer, Readra tells you so instead of guessing.

04

Measured, not assumed.

Retrieval is scored against a labelled question set, and every answer is checked against the quoted source before you see it.
